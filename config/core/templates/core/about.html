Our Mission

We believe that technical hiring and skill verification should be fair, practical, and transparent. Traditional multiple-choice tests fail to measure real-world engineering capability.

By combining advanced AI evaluation with realistic coding environments and immutable blockchain credentials, we empower developers to showcase their true potential while giving employers cryptographically verifiable proof of those skills.

How It Works

1. Choose a Skill

Select from our catalog of over 50 technical skills covering frontend, backend, cloud, and AI.

1

2. Generate AI Assessment

Gemini AI generates a unique, un-cheatable set of 5 practical challenges tailored to the skill.

2

3. Complete Practical Tasks

Write real code, architecture designs, or technical explanations in our long-form editor.

Pass the assessment and receive a beautifully designed, downloadable PDF certificate.
