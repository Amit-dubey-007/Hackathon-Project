Help & Troubleshooting

How does local AI proctoring work?

The anti-cheat runs 100% locally in your browser. It uses MediaPipe and TensorFlow.js to scan webcam feeds without uploading files to clouds. Ensure you grant camera permissions before starting.

What is a Soulbound Token (SBT)?

Soulbound tokens are non-transferable NFTs issued to your wallet to establish verified on-chain cryptographic proofs of your assessments.

Having camera errors?

Check if other applications are using the camera. Refresh the browser and select "Allow Camera Permissions" when prompted.
