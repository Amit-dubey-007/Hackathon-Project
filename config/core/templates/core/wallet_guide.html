Web3 Integration

Complete Wallet Guide

Everything you need to know about setting up MetaMask, connecting to the platform, and minting your skill assessment certificates as NFTs.

Why Connect a Web3 Wallet?

NFT Certificate

Your achievement is minted as a permanent, soulbound NFT token on the blockchain.

Blockchain Verification

Employers can cryptographically verify that you, and only you, earned this specific score.

True Ownership

You hold the private keys. The platform cannot revoke or alter your certificate once minted.

Downloadable Credential

Alongside the NFT, you get a beautiful PDF certificate with an embedded verification QR code.

How to Mint Your Certificate

1. Install MetaMask

Download the official MetaMask extension for Chrome, Firefox, or Brave.

1

2. Create Wallet

Follow the setup instructions to create a new wallet and secure it with a strong password.

2

3. Backup Secret Phrase

Write down your 12-word seed phrase on paper. Never share this with anyone, including us.

3

4. Add Sepolia Network

In MetaMask settings, enable 'Show test networks' and switch to the Sepolia test network.

4

You need Sepolia ETH to pay for minting gas. Use a public faucet like Alchemy or Infura to get free test ETH.

5

6. Connect Wallet

6

7. Pass Assessment

7

8. Mint Certificate

Click 'Mint NFT' on your results page. MetaMask will prompt you to confirm the transaction and pay gas.

8

Troubleshooting

Make sure the MetaMask extension is installed and unlocked. Try refreshing the page. If you are using Brave browser, ensure the default Web3 wallet is set to MetaMask or Brave Wallet.

Our smart contracts are deployed on Sepolia. Open MetaMask, click the network dropdown at the top, toggle "Show test networks", and select Sepolia.

Minting requires a tiny amount of ETH to pay the network gas fee. You can get free Sepolia ETH from the official Sepolia Faucet or Alchemy Faucet.

If your transaction is pending for more than 5 minutes, you can speed it up in MetaMask by increasing the gas fee. If it failed, check Etherscan for the revert reason (usually out of gas).

Security Tips

Never share your seed phrase. Support will never ask for it.

Verify URLs. Always ensure you are on the correct domain before connecting.

Use official wallets. Only download MetaMask from metamask.io.

Don't approve unknown contracts. Read transactions carefully before signing.
